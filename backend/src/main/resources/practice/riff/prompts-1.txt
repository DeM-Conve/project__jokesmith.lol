# Daily Riff prompts, part 1. Format: "> setup" then "- punchline" lines; blank line between entries. APPEND ONLY.

> My landlord just texted "we need to talk."
- That's the most he's communicated since the boiler broke in 2019.
- I've started packing. Not to leave, just emotionally.
- He's never needed to talk. He's needed to un-fix a radiator, he's needed to raise the rent, but talk? This is new territory.
- I replied "ok" and he left me on read for six hours, which I think means I'm now the one who owes him money.
- Turns out he wanted to talk about my "energy in the stairwell."

> My mum just discovered voice notes.
- Each one opens with forty seconds of her asking if it's recording.
- She sends them like podcasts. Eleven minutes, three segments, a guest appearance from the dog.
- She sent me one that was just her saying "never mind, I'll text you," and then she texted me the same thing.
- I listen at 2x speed and she still sounds like she's being interviewed by the police.
- Half of them end with her shouting at Dad because she thinks the phone has hung up.

- Someone in the Dubai office I've never met replied "lol same."
- The only person who didn't read it was the one I meant to send it to.

- It came with a photo of the treadmill I used once. It's moved on. It looks happy.
- They're not missing me. They're cashing me.
